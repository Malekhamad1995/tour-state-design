Tour Estate — Mobile App: every screen

One photograph of every screen of the classic app, for each kind of user, in each case that changes how it looks (empty, loading, failed, refused, filled, a trip running…), in English and Arabic side by side. It is made for a designer to read from top to bottom and write notes against: there is a notes box under every case, and “Export my notes” turns them into one file to send back.

صورة لكل شاشة في تطبيق الموبايل، لكل نوع مستخدم (زائر، مسافر، طاقم)، وبكل الحالات التي تغيّر شكل الشاشة (فارغة، تحميل، فشل، رفض، ممتلئة…) بالإنجليزية والعربية جنبًا إلى جنب. تحت كل حالة مربع للملاحظات، وزر «Export my notes» يجمعها في ملف واحد للإرسال.

How it was photographed
  • The real app (Flutter web build of Mobile App/Project, the classic app — not the Luxe copy) on a 375 × 812 phone canvas at 2×, in the real Chrome-family engine. What you see is what the code draws today, not a mock-up.
  • Catalogue content (tours, photos, prices, developers, legal text) is the live dev server's. The traveller and crew data are invented for this document (a made-up traveller “Layla Haddad”, made-up bookings and trips) so nothing was written to the shared server.
  • A case the server cannot show on demand (empty, slow, failed, refused) was forced by answering that one request differently — it is the app's real reaction to that answer.
  • Scroll positions are separate shots, labelled “scrolled 1, 2…”, for screens longer than the phone.
What to keep in mind while reviewing
  • The map screens show pins and routes on a plain background: the build has no Mapbox token, so the street basemap is missing here (it is drawn on a real phone).
  • The intro video on the first screen appears here as a still frame.
  • The phone's own pop-ups (photo picker, share sheet, permission prompts) are not photographed.
  • Prices, totals and dates on the traveller and crew screens are sample figures made up for this document — review the look, not the numbers.
